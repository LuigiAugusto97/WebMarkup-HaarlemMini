Your Hobby Starts Here

What Do I Need to Start?

You don't need a huge collection of supplies to start painting miniatures. With a few basic materials and tools, you can build, prepare and paint your first model.

Paints

Miniature paints are made specifically for small models and come in many different types. Base paints are used for the main colours, washes add shadows and depth, and metallic paints create metal effects. Start with a small selection of colours rather than buying a large collection.

What colours should I get?

A few basic colours

Start with the main colours you expect to use most, such as red, blue, yellow, green and brown.

Black and white

Use these to darken, lighten and mix different tones without needing to buy every shade separately.

One or two washes

Washes settle into the model's details and create quick shadows, making the miniature look more three-dimensional.

A metallic colour

A silver or gold paint is useful for details such as weapons, armour, buckles and other metal parts.

Brushes

You don't need many brushes when you're starting. A few good-quality brushes in different sizes are enough for most beginner projects. Larger brushes work well for bigger areas, while smaller brushes are useful for details.

What brushes should I get?

Medium/Regular brush

Ideal for applying base colours to larger areas, such as clothing, armour and skin.

Small brush

Useful for painting smaller areas and details without accidentally covering nearby colours.

Fine brush

Designed for tiny details such as eyes, buttons, symbols and small highlights.

Dry brush

A brush with stiff bristles used with very little paint to quickly highlight raised areas and textures, such as fur, armour and stone.

Essential Tools

A few simple tools make assembling and preparing miniatures much easier. You don't need professional equipment to get started.

What tools are needed?

Essential miniature preparation and painting tools
Tool What is it used for?
Clippers Removing pieces from the sprue
Knife Cleaning mould lines and small imperfections
Files Smoothing rough edges
Palette Mixing and thinning paint
Water cup Cleaning brushes while painting
Cutting mat Protecting your workspace
