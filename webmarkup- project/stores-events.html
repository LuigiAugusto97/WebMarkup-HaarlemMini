Discover Haarlem's Hobby Community

Find the Hobby in Haarlem

You don't have to explore the hobby alone. Haarlem has several shops where you can discover tabletop games, find miniatures and buy the materials you need to start painting. Some stores also offer places to play, events and opportunities to meet other hobbyists.

Where to Buy

Whether you're looking for your first miniature, new paints or tools for your next project, Haarlem has a range of specialist shops worth exploring. Some focus on tabletop games and miniatures, while others offer model-building supplies and materials.

Local Stores

Here are some places in Haarlem where you can explore the world of miniatures, tabletop games and model building.

D6 Gaming

A local games store offering tabletop games, Dungeons & Dragons, Warhammer products and other gaming supplies. It also has a gaming space where visitors can join events and meet other players.

Schagchelstraat 6, Haarlem
Visit D6 Gaming

Tabletop Kingdom

A specialist games shop selling board games, miniature games and related products. It also organises events for different games, making it a place to discover new tabletop hobbies.

Kleine Houtstraat 26, Haarlem
Visit Tabletop Kingdom

Spellenhuis

A board game shop with a cafe where visitors can try games and play together. Although it is not specifically a miniature-painting store, it is a good place to discover tabletop gaming and meet other players.

Kruisweg 60, Haarlem
Visit Spellenhuis
